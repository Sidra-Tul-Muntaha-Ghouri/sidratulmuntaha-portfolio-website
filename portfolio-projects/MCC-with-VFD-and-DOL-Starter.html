MCC with VFD and DOL Starter

Switchgear Panel · PrismaSet International

This is a Motor Control Center (MCC) panel with VFD and DOL starters and an Auto Transfer Switch (ATS). This panel is designed in PrismaSet International Enclosure, with Form 2B and IP-54 rating. The drawing consists of the General Arrangement drawing, Schedmatic drawing, Control wiring, Terminal layout, and Bill on Materials.
