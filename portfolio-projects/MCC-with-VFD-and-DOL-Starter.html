MCC with VFD and DOL Starter

Switchgear Panel · PrismaSet International

This project involved the detailed electrical design and documentation of a Motor Control Center (MCC) designed for The Address Hotel & Service Apartments. The panel is a 400/230 V, 3-phase, 4-wire system operating at 60 Hz and was designed as a PrismaSet International switchboard with Form 2B internal separation.

The design incorporates dedicated motor starters, variable frequency drive (VFD) feeders, direct-on-line (DOL) starters, automatic transfer functionality, metering, protection, control, and Building Management System (BMS) interfaces.

For monitoring and integration, the panel includes a digital metering system with voltage and current measurement and an RS-485 communication interface, which enables the panel to provide operational and status information to higher-level monitoring and BMS.

The engineering documentation covers the complete design package, including the General Characteristics, Panel Layout, Nameplate Details, Client SLD, Incoming and Outgoing Schematics, VFD Starter Schematics, DOL Starter Schematics, Control Circuits, Door Limit Switches, Heater Circuit, BMS Signals, Terminal Diagrams, and Material List.

The design documentation specifies compliance with IEC 61439-1 & IEC 61439-2 and incorporates defined enclosure, protection, labeling, and wiring requirements.
