About me

I'm an Electrical Design Engineer with over 5 years of hands-on experience designing low-voltage switchgear panels, including including Motor Control Centers (MCCs), Power Control Centers (PCCs), Power Factor Correction panels (PFCs), and Distribution Boards (DBs). I've contributed to 50+ industrial projects, designing 700+ electrical panels with current ratings up to 5000A using EPLAN Electric P8 and AutoCAD.

My experience includes component selection, busbar sizing, cable sizing, technical documentation, and engineering design in accordance with IEC standards. I have a proven ability to lead engineering teams, collaborate with international clients, and deliver large-scale switchgear projects.
